**12. Describe your product**
```
Verifiable MUSD statements, invoice-referenced payments and onchain proof. MUSD Books turns any Mezo address into an exact MUSD statement that reconciles to the last unit, attaches invoice numbers to MUSD payments through MusdPay, and anchors the statement hash onchain, so an accountant, lender or customer can verify it from chain data without trusting the sender.
```

**13. Which protocols and ecosystems are you currently live on**
```
Mezo mainnet (31612, since 13 Nov 2026) and Mezo testnet (31611), contracts verified on both explorers. Built on MUSD: permit payments, Trove repay and add-collateral through BorrowerOperations, Stability Pool deposits. Reads MEZO and other ERC-20s and labels Tigris pools and Wormhole NTT. Not live on other chains.
```

**16. How did you hear about the program?**
```
Through the Mezo Buildathon on AKINDO (MUSD Books took part in Wave 1 and Wave 2) and the Supernormal Foundation website.
```
