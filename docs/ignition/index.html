MUSD Books

Verifiable books for MUSD: statements, invoice references and onchain proof.

Live on Mezo mainnet since 13 Nov 2026 · open source (MIT) · neimasilk.github.io/musd-books

The problem

Mezo lets people borrow MUSD against Bitcoin instead of selling it, and MUSD is a stablecoin people can pay and get paid in. The borrower, the business paid in MUSD, and their accountant or lender then all need the same record, and it has to be one a third party can check.

Explorers show raw logs. Nobody can hand an auditor a log.
Spreadsheets from one party prove nothing to another party.
A transfer has no invoice number, so matching payments to orders is manual.

What exists today

Statement engine (browser only, no backend). Every Transfer counted once in BigInt, reconciled to the last unit against balanceOf at the opening and closing blocks. Mint = loan draw, burn = repayment, core Mezo contracts named.
MusdPay. Moves MUSD payer → merchant and emits a PaymentReference (invoice number) right after the token's Transfer. One transaction with permit. No owner, no fee.
StatementRegistry. The hash of the deterministic CSV is anchored onchain; accountant and owner can co-attest the same hash. Anyone can re-derive the statement from chain data.
Act on Mezo. Next to the statement, a Trove owner can repay MUSD, add BTC collateral, and deposit to or withdraw from the Stability Pool, with a preview that refuses what the protocol would reject.

Why this matters for Mezo

  • MUSD as money needs books. A business that accepts MUSD has to show its accountant what came in. Without a checkable record, MUSD stays something people only trade.
  • Borrowers need proof for lenders. A Trove owner can hand over a statement plus a live Trove report that the lender verifies without trusting the borrower.
  • Free public infrastructure. The contracts take no fee and have no owner, so any Mezo wallet, merchant tool or dApp can use them. Revenue, later, comes from optional tools around them (accountant workspace, merchant tooling), paid in MUSD through MusdPay.
  • Nothing like it on Mezo yet. Explorers show logs; MUSD Books gives a reconciled statement and an invoice trail that a third party can verify.
