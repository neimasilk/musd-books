MUSD Books

Verifiable books for MUSD: statements, invoice references and onchain proof.

Live on Mezo Testnet · open source (MIT) · neimasilk.github.io/musd-books

The problem

Mezo lets people borrow MUSD against Bitcoin instead of selling it, and MUSD is a stablecoin people can pay and get paid in. Three people then need the same record: the borrower, the business paid in MUSD, and their accountant or lender.

Explorers show raw logs. Nobody can hand an auditor a log.
Spreadsheets from one party prove nothing to another party.
A transfer has no invoice number, so matching payments to orders is manual.

What MUSD Books does

Exact statement. Every MUSD Transfer once, in BigInt, reconciled to the last unit against balanceOf at the opening and closing blocks.
Mezo-aware. Mint = loan draw, burn = repayment. BorrowerOperations, StabilityPool, PCV and others are named.
Invoice references. MusdPay attaches a reference to a payment. One transaction with permit.
Proof. The CSV is deterministic. Its hash is anchored in StatementRegistry. Anyone can re-derive it from chain data.

How it fits together

A reference can never attach to the wrong payment: the engine joins PaymentReference to the transfer by log position and requires payer, merchant and amount to match.

Deep MUSD integration

  • Real MUSD (OpenZeppelin ERC20Permit): single-transaction payments signed with EIP-2612.
  • Real Trove flows: openTrove, withdrawMUSD, repayMUSD appear as mint and burn rows, with Mezo's own minimum-debt rule respected. A live Trove panel (collateral, debt, interest, collateral ratio) is read from TroveManager and the price feed.
  • Any ERC-20, so MEZO and Mezo-bridged tokens work too.
  • Mainnet-ready reading: the same engine already reconciles real mainnet MUSD accounts with zero difference.

Why it is credible

  • Reproducible. 13 Foundry unit tests, a read-only live test against Mezo mainnet, an end-to-end script that ran on a fork and on testnet, and a scripted-wallet test of the Pay page (permit and approve paths) on testnet.
  • Deterministic. Same inputs, same bytes. The CSV header holds everything needed to re-derive it.
  • Honest limits. Bookkeeping aid, not tax advice. One token per statement. Native BTC gas is out of scope.
  • Prior art by the same author: ArcBooks, the same idea on Arc, with a live mainnet deployment. MUSD Books is a new engine for Mezo's ERC-20 and Trove model.
